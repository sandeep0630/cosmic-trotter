Who Is Lord Hanuman?

హనుమంత స్వరూపం & 11వ రుద్రావతారం • The 11th Rudra Incarnation

Lord Hanuman — the master of vital energy, supreme intelligence, and selfless service.

The 11th Rudra Avatara of Lord Shiva

In the vast pantheon of Vedic literature and epic tradition, **Lord Hanuman** stands as a unique, towering figure: He is simultaneously the supreme warrior, the master of all 64 arts, the greatest grammarian, and the quintessential humble servant (**Dasa**). Puranic literature, including the *Siva Purana* and *Shatapatha Brahmana*, reveals that Hanuman is the **Eleventh Rudra Avatara** of Lord Shiva, born to assist Lord Vishnu during His descent as Sri Rama.

When Lord Vishnu decided to descend as Rama to establish *Dharma* and defeat Ravana, Lord Shiva declared: "Where my Lord Vishnu goes as a mortal king, I shall follow as His humble servant." Shiva projected His invincible Rudra energy into the womb of **Anjana Devi**, born through the divine vehicle of **Vayu Deva** (the Wind God). Thus, Hanuman carries the supreme strength of Shiva and the swiftness of Vayu.

Traditional layer: Shiva Purana (Rudra Samhita) & Valmiki Ramayana.

Names of Nearness and Divine Attributes

Devotees call Him by names of tender love as well as cosmic power:

  • Anjaneya: The glorious son of Mother Anjana.
  • Maruti & Pavanaputra: The son of Vayu Deva, born of the cosmic wind element (*Prana*).
  • Bajrangbali: One whose limbs are as unyielding as a diamond (*Vajra*) and possess supreme strength.
  • Sankat Mochan: The compassionate dispeller of every human crisis, illness, and fear.
  • Ramadoota: The trusted messenger and eternal ambassador of Lord Rama.

The Synthesis of Intelligence and Humility

In Brahmasri Chaganti Koteswara Rao's famous discourses on *Sundarakanda*, he highlights the immortal Dhyana Shloka that summarizes Hanuman's spirit:

"Manojavam Marutatulyavegam Jitendriyam Buddhimatam Varishtam | Vatatmajam Vanarayuthamukhyam Sriramadutam Sharanam Prapadye ||"
(I surrender to Ramaduta Hanuman — swift as the mind, fast as the wind, master of senses, supreme among the intelligent, and chief of the Vanara army!)

Chaganti Garu points out that Hanuman never boasts of His infinite power. When He performs the impossible 100-yojana ocean leap, burns Lanka, or carries the Sanjeevani mountain, He bows His head and declares: "This is not my strength; it is the grace of Sri Rama's name operating through this humble body!"

Core Wisdom
  • • 11th Rudra: Lord Shiva's divine energy taking form to serve Lord Rama.
  • • Prana Tattva: Hanuman as the master of cosmic breath (*Vayu*), bestowing vitality and health.
  • • Jitendriya: Complete mastery over senses and ego, transforming raw power into divine service.
  • • Buddhimatam Varishtam: Supreme intellect governed by profound humility and love.

Pre-Historical Birth & Solar Encounter

జననం & సూర్య మండల ప్రయాణం • Child Hanuman Leaping Toward the Sun

Anjanadri — Child Hanuman leaping into the cosmic sky toward the rising sun.

Anjana's Tapas & The Divine Payasam

In Treta Yuga, on the sacred Anjanadri hill (at Kishkindha / Tirumala range), **Mother Anjana** and her husband **Kesari** performed thousands of years of intense penance to beget a child of unmatchable strength and divine virtue. Lord Shiva, pleased by their devotion, granted the boon.

At that precise time in Ayodhya, King Dasharatha performed the *Putrakameshti Yajna* and received divine *Payasam* (sacred sweet rice) from the Yajna Purusha. By divine ordinance, Vayu Deva (the Wind God) carried a portion of the sacred Payasam across the sky and deposited it into Anjana's outstretched hands as she meditated. Consuming the Payasam, Anjana gave birth to child Hanuman on the auspicious day of *Chaitra Purnima*.

Leaping Toward the Sun & Indra's Thunderbolt

One morning, child Hanuman woke up feeling ravenously hungry. Looking toward the eastern horizon, He beheld the glowing red rising Sun (**Surya Deva**). Thinking the radiant orb to be a giant, ripe, delicious red fruit (*Phala*), the divine child sprang off the mountain peak and leaped into the cosmic sky!

Flying millions of miles through the atmosphere, child Hanuman approached Surya Deva. Rahu, the cosmic eclipse demon who had come to devour the Sun on that day, fled in terror upon seeing child Hanuman. Rahu complained to **Indra**, the King of Gods. Riding his elephant Airavata, Indra hurled his cosmic thunderbolt (Vajrayudha) at the child. The bolt struck Hanuman on His left jaw (Hanu), knocking Him unconscious onto the mountain.

Vayu's Anger & The Cosmic Boons

Enraged by the attack on His divine child, **Vayu Deva** withdrew the cosmic breath (*Prana*) from all fourteen worlds! Humans, gods, sages, and animals collapsed, gasping for air. Panicked, Lord Brahma, Lord Vishnu, Lord Shiva, and all celestial deities rushed to Anjanadri.

Lord Brahma revived child Hanuman with a touch. To pacify Vayu Deva, every deity showered the child with supreme cosmic boons:

  • Brahma: Granted invulnerability to any weapon, freedom from death by curses, and the power to change size at will (*Kamarupa*).
  • Indra: Declared that his Vajra would never harm Him again, naming Him **Hanuman** (He of the Sacred Jaw).
  • Varuna & Agni: Granted immunity from water and fire.
  • Yama & Surya: Granted freedom from death (*Chiranjeevi*) and promised 100 times the brightness of the Sun.
Core Wisdom
  • • Divine Payasam: Hanuman's birth linked directly to the cosmic Yajna of Rama's arrival.
  • • Solar Leap: The child's fearless leap symbolizing the soul's natural attraction to ultimate light.
  • • Cosmic Boons: All celestial powers uniting to forge an invulnerable guardian of Dharma.
  • • Forgetfulness Spell: Sages veiling His power so He remains a humble seeker until awakened.

Education Under Surya Deva & Meeting Lord Rama

సూర్యోపాసన & శ్రీరామ దర్శనం • The Master Grammarian meets the Divine Prince

Rishyamooka Hill — Hanuman meeting Lord Rama and Lakshmana for the first time.

Surya Deva as Guru — The Backward Chariot Walk

When it came time for Hanuman's education, He approached **Surya Deva** (the Sun God) to be His Guru. Surya Deva hesitated: "My child, I travel unceasingly across the sky in my chariot without stopping for a single second. How can I teach you?"

Hanuman smiled, expanded His form to equal the Sun, and faced Surya Deva's chariot. Walking backward across the sky continuously while facing His Guru, Hanuman mastered the **4 Vedas, 6 Vedangas, 60 Shastras, and all 64 arts** in just 60 days! Never once did He turn His back on His Guru. Surprised by His genius, Surya Deva refused to accept tuition fees (*Guru Dakshina*), asking only that Hanuman protect his spiritual son **Sugriva** at Kishkindha.

Traditional layer: Valmiki Ramayana (Kishkindha Kanda) & Parasara Samhita.

First Meeting at Rishyamooka Hill

When Lord Rama and Lakshmana entered the forests of Kishkindha searching for Sita Devi, Sugriva saw the two armed princes from Rishyamooka hill and grew fearful, suspecting them to be assassins sent by his brother Vali. Sugriva sent Hanuman in disguise to test their intentions.

Hanuman assumed the form of a serene Brahmana scholar, approached the divine brothers, and greeted them with melodious Sanskrit shlokas of sublime beauty. Hearing Hanuman speak, Lord Rama turned to Lakshmana in marvel:

"Naanrigveda Vineetasya Naayurvedavidho Tapi | Naasaamavedavidha Sakya Mevam Vibhashitum ||"
(O Lakshmana, no person who has not mastered the Rigveda, Yajurveda, and Samaveda could speak with such flawless grammar and sweetness! His speech contains no error, no posture of pride. He is a master of grammar!)

The Sacred Covenant of Friendship

Realizing who they were, Hanuman cast off His disguise, bowed at Sri Rama's lotus feet, and placed Rama and Lakshmana upon His shoulders, carrying them up the mountain to meet Sugriva. Before a sacred fire, Hanuman forged the eternal bond of friendship (*Mitram*) between Sri Rama and Sugriva, setting in motion the liberation of Sugriva's kingdom and the search for Sita Devi.

Core Wisdom
  • • Guru Bhakti: Walking backward across the cosmos to master wisdom without turning away from the Sun.
  • • Master of Speech: Speech that is free from anger, ego, and grammatical flaw opens every divine door.
  • • True Alliance: Connecting noble souls through selfless mediation and truth.
  • • Ramaduta Awakening: The moment Hanuman finds His eternal purpose at Rama's feet.

The Epic Ocean Leap & Sundarakanda

సుందరకాండ & సముద్ర లంఘనం • The 100-Yojana Leap and Finding Sita Devi

Mahendra Giri — Hanuman expanding into His Vishwaroopa form to cross the 100-yojana ocean.

Jambavan's Awakening on Mahendra Giri

When the Vanara search party reached the southern shore of India at Mahendra Giri mountain, they stood despondent before the vast, roaring 100-yojana (800-mile) ocean. Angada, Nila, and Jambavan discussed who could leap across. Angada said he could leap 100 yojanas but doubted if he had strength to return. Seeing Hanuman sitting quietly in meditation nearby, the wise old bear **Jambavan** approached Him and recited His forgotten divine lineage:

"Why are you silent, O powerful son of Vayu? You possess the strength of Rudra, the speed of the wind, and the wisdom of the gods! Stand up and fulfill the mission of Sri Rama!"

Upon hearing Rama's name and His divine lineage, the spell of forgetfulness broke! Hanuman roared like cosmic thunder, expanded into a mountain-like **Vishwaroopa**, pressed Mahendra Giri into the earth with His feet, and launched Himself into the sky like a divine arrow!

Overcoming the Three Ocean Trials

During the epic flight across the ocean, three cosmic powers tested Hanuman:

  • Mainaka Mountain: Rose from the sea offering golden peaks for rest. Hanuman touched Mainaka respectfully but refused to rest, saying: "Until Rama's work is accomplished, there is no rest for me!"
  • Surasa (Mother of Nagas): Sent by the gods to test His intellect, she expanded her jaws to swallow Him. Hanuman expanded to 100 yojanas, then shrank to the size of a thumb, entered her mouth, and popped out in a split second — winning her blessing through agility over brute force!
  • Sinhika (Shadow Demoness): Grabbed Hanuman's shadow from the ocean waters below. Hanuman dived into her throat, tore her heart out, and destroyed the phantom demoness.

Finding Mother Sita at Ashoka Vatika

Ashoka Vatika — Hanuman presenting the royal signet ring (Rama Angulyakam) to Sita Devi.

Arriving in Lanka at night in microscopic form (*Sukshma Rupa*), Hanuman searched every palace and garden until He reached **Ashoka Vatika**. There, under a *Simshapam* tree, He saw Goddess Sita — thin, heartbroken, surrounded by demonesses, yet holding her mind steadfastly on Sri Rama.

To avoid scaring her, Hanuman perched on a high branch and softly sang the entire story of Sri Rama's lineage in sweet, pure Sanskrit. When Sita looked up in wonder, Hanuman descended, bowed with folded hands, and presented the royal signet ring (**Rama Angulyakam**). Receiving Sita's crest jewel (**Chudamani**) in return, Hanuman's mission transformed from search to absolute triumph!

Core Wisdom
  • • Sundarakanda: The chapter of beauty and hope where human effort backed by devotion turns defeat into victory.
  • • Mainaka Test: True seekers do not pause for comfortable rest until the divine goal is reached.
  • • Surasa Test: Adapting size and intelligence overcomes obstacles that brute force cannot solve.
  • • Rama Angulyakam: Sacred words and tokens of love that heal despair and restore faith.

Lanka Dahanam & Sanjeevani Mountain

లంకా దహనం & సంజీవని పర్వతం • The Fiery Tail and Himalayan Mountain Peak

Lanka Dahanam — Hanuman reducing the pride of Ravana's golden fortress to ash.

Lanka Dahanam — The Fiery Tail

Before leaving Lanka, Hanuman decided to test Ravana's military strength. He destroyed the royal Madhuvana garden, defeated Ravana's son Jambumali, and killed 80,000 demon warriors. When Indrajit launched the Brahmastra, Hanuman allowed Himself to be bound out of respect for Lord Brahma's weapon and was dragged into Ravana's royal court.

Standing fearless before the ten-headed demon king, Hanuman warned Ravana to return Sita Devi and surrender to Rama. Enraged, Ravana ordered Hanuman's execution, but Vibhishana intervened, noting that an ambassador cannot be killed. Ravana ordered Hanuman's tail to be wrapped in cloth, soaked in oil, and set ablaze.

Hanuman expanded His tail indefinitely, consuming all of Lanka's oil reserves! As the flames caught, Hanuman shrank His body, escaped His bonds, leaped from rooftop to rooftop, and reduced the golden fortress of Lanka to ash — while Agni Deva kept the fire cool around Hanuman by Sita's blessing!

Carrying the Sanjeevani Mountain

Dronagiri — Hanuman carrying the entire Himalayan mountain peak before sunrise to save Lakshmana.

During the great war in Lanka, Lakshmana was struck by Indrajit's deadly *Nagastra / Shakti spear* and fell unconscious on the battlefield. The physician Sushena declared that Lakshmana could only be revived before sunrise by the rare life-giving herb **Mritasanjeevani**, found on the distant **Dronagiri mountain** in the Himalayas.

Hanuman leaped thousands of miles to the Himalayas before midnight. Upon reaching Dronagiri, the divine herbs hid themselves in light. Realizing there was no time to search herb by herb as dawn approached, Hanuman placed His hands underneath the mountain peak, lifted the entire **Dronagiri Mountain** on His single palm, and flew back across India! Sushena applied the Sanjeevani herb, Lakshmana revived, and the Vanara army cheered in ecstasy.

Core Wisdom
  • • Lanka Dahanam: The fire of arrogance burns down its own house; pure devotion remains untouched.
  • • Dronagiri Feat: When time is short and task is critical, lift the whole mountain of obstacles at once.
  • • Sanjeevani Grace: Bringing life and healing breath (*Prana*) to those in mortal peril.
  • • Fearlessness: Standing before tyrant power with unshakeable faith in Sri Rama.

Panchamukha Hanuman & Patala Loka Victory

పంచముఖ ఆంజనేయ స్వరూపం • The Five Cosmic Faces and Extinguishing the Five Lamps

Panchamukha Hanuman — the cosmic form combining Hanuman, Narasimha, Garuda, Varaha, and Hayagriva.

Ahiravana's Abduction & Descent to Patala

In the final phase of the Lanka war, Ravana summoned his brother **Ahiravana**, the dark king of the subterranean realm (**Patala Loka**). Ahiravana was a master of dark sorcery. Disguising himself as Vibhishana, Ahiravana entered the Vanara camp at night, placed Rama and Lakshmana into a magical sleep, and carried them down to Patala Loka to sacrifice them to Goddess Mahamaya.

Discovering the abduction, Hanuman extended His tail into a giant fortress wall around the army, created a subterranean tunnel with His mace, and descended into the depths of Patala Loka to rescue His Lord.

Manifestation of Panchamukha Hanuman (Five Faces)

In Patala Loka, Hanuman discovered that Ahiravana's life was tied to **five cosmic lamps** burning in five different directions. To kill Ahiravana, all five lamps had to be extinguished simultaneously in one breath!

To accomplish this, Hanuman manifested His supreme cosmic form — **Panchamukha Anjaneya (Five-Faced Hanuman)**:

  1. Sri Hanuman (East): Facing East — grants purity of mind, destroys sins, and bestows devotion.
  2. Sri Narasimha (South): Facing South — fierce lion face that destroys fear, enemies, and evil spirits.
  3. Sri Garuda (West): Facing West — celestial eagle face that neutralizes all poisons, snakebites, and diseases.
  4. Sri Varaha (North): Facing North — divine boar face that grants wealth, land, and planetary peace.
  5. Sri Hayagriva (Sky/Upward): Facing Sky — horse-headed face that bestows supreme Vedantic wisdom and victory in education.

Discourse research: Sri Nanduri Srinivas's lectures on Panchamukha Stotram detail how chanting Panchamukha Kavacham protects against all 5 directions of karmic affliction.

Extinguishing the Lamps & Rescue of Rama

With His five cosmic faces blowing simultaneously in five directions, Panchamukha Hanuman extinguished all five lamps in a single instant! Ahiravana's power collapsed, Hanuman slew the sorcerer king, carried Sri Rama and Lakshmana upon His shoulders back to Earth, and restored them to the victorious army.

Core Wisdom
  • • Panchamukha Form: Integrating the protective powers of Hanuman, Narasimha, Garuda, Varaha, and Hayagriva.
  • • Simultaneous Action: Blowing out 5 directions of evil at once through multidimensional awareness.
  • • Patala Rescue: No realm — however deep or dark — is beyond the reach of Hanuman's saving grace.
  • • Panchamukha Kavacham: The supreme protective shield for body, mind, and spirit.

Ashta Siddhi, Nava Nidhi & Hanuman Chalisa

అష్టసిద్ధి & హనుమాన్ చాలీసా • Mother Sita's Boons and Tulsidas's 40 Verses

Mother Sita's Boon — Ashta Siddhi & Nava Nidhi

After the coronation of Sri Rama in Ayodhya (*Rama Rajya*), Goddess Sita presented gifts to all Vanara heroes. When Hanuman approached, Sita possessed no jewel equal to His devotion. Mother Sita placed her hand on Hanuman's head and granted Him the supreme cosmic boon:

"Ashta Siddhi Nava Nidhi Ke Data | Asavar Deenhi Janki Mata ||"
(You are the eternal giver of the Eight Supernatural Powers and the Nine Cosmic Treasures, blessed by Mother Janki!)

The **Ashta Siddhis (8 Powers)** include: *Anima* (reducing to atomic size), *Mahima* (expanding to mountain size), *Garima* (becoming infinitely heavy), *Laghima* (becoming weightless as wind), *Prapti* (attaining any object instantly), *Prakamya* (fulfilling any desire), *Ishitva* (supreme divine lordship), and *Vashitva* (mastery over all nature).

The Tear in the Chest — Rama in Every Atom

When Sita presented Hanuman a necklace of priceless pearls, Hanuman took each pearl between His teeth, broke it open, and examined it. When court ministers mocked Him, asking what He was looking for, Hanuman replied: "I am looking to see if Sri Rama's name is written inside these pearls. Any jewel that does not contain Rama is worthless to me!"

When the court laughed in disbelief, Hanuman used His sharp claws to tear open His own chest. Inside His glowing heart, Sri Rama and Sita Devi were revealed seated upon a golden throne, radiating divine light! The entire assembly fell at Hanuman's feet in tears of ecstasy.

Sant Tulsidas & The 40 Verses of Hanuman Chalisa

In the 16th century, **Sant Tulsidas** was imprisoned in Kashi by Emperor Akbar after refusing to perform artificial magic tricks. Sitting inside his cell, Tulsidas composed the 40 verses of the **Hanuman Chalisa** in Awadhi Hindi.

Upon completing the 40th verse, millions of monkeys swarmed the royal palace in Kashi, jumping on walls and roofs until the emperor freed Tulsidas and apologized! To this day, chanting the Hanuman Chalisa is practiced by millions daily across India to overcome fear, illness, depression, and planetary afflictions.

Core Wisdom
  • • Ashta Siddhi: Master of 8 yogic powers, using them solely for divine service.
  • • Rama in the Heart: Material wealth is worthless unless charged with divine love.
  • • Hanuman Chalisa: 40 verses of protective spiritual energy for daily life.
  • • Sankat Mochan: Transforming human weakness into divine strength through prayer.

Living Niti, Tail Worship & The Chiranjeevi Vow

వాలపూజా రహస్యం & నిత్య చిరంజీవిత్వం • The Eternal Presence on Earth

Tail Worship (Vala Pooja / Taila Abhishekam)

As detailed by Sri Nanduri Srinivas in his lectures on *Hanuman Upasana Secrets*, one of the most effective traditional worship practices is **Vala Pooja (Tail Worship)**. In iconography, Hanuman's tail loops high above His head carrying a ringing bell or lotus flower, representing the spine (*Sushumna Nadi*) and Kundalini energy.

Devotees apply fresh Sindoor (vermilion) and Jasmine oil starting from the base of Hanuman's tail up to its tip for 40 consecutive days. This practice is believed to bestow victory in legal disputes, protection against negative eye (*Drishti*), and unshakable courage in times of crisis.

The Eternal Chiranjeevi Vow

When Lord Rama completed His earthly avatar and prepared to return to Vaikuntha, He invited Hanuman to join Him. Hanuman bowed and asked: "My Lord, will your divine story (*Rama Katha*) be sung in Vaikuntha?" Rama replied: "In Vaikuntha, there is only eternal peace; my earthly story is sung on Earth."

Hanuman requested: "Then grant me the boon to remain on Earth as a Chiranjeevi (immortal) as long as your holy name is chanted by human lips!" Sri Rama embraced Hanuman, granting the eternal vow. Puranas declare that wherever Sri Rama's name is chanted — in temples, homes, or quiet forests — Lord Hanuman stands there unseen with folded hands, tears of joy streaming from His eyes!

How to Walk This Storybook Journey

To approach Hanuman is to learn that physical strength without humility is arrogance, but strength dedicated to God is salvation. Whether chanting the Hanuman Chalisa, reading Sundarakanda, or serving those in need, Hanuman's presence promises: "Where there is faith, fear cannot stand."

Core Wisdom
  • • Vala Pooja: Worshipping the tail energy for courage, health, and Kundalini awakening.
  • • Chiranjeevi Presence: Hanuman living physically on Earth wherever Ramayana is sung.
  • • Fearlessness: Realizing that divine protection surrounds every sincere heart.
  • • Dasa Bhakti: Master of all arts who chooses the glory of humble service.
